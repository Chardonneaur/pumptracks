What the data will never hold

No open dataset says whether the surface has cracked, whether the rollers still stand, whether the place is worth the trip on a Sunday. For that, someone has to have been there. If you know one of these 816 pumptracks — or one that is missing — every page carries a link to say so.

What this site refuses to do

A page states only what a source declares. No assumed surface, no “closed” inferred from silence: a missing field was never filled in, and does not mean “no”. Every page names its source in plain sight.

181 entries have no position in either source: their point is the centre of their commune, and they say so.

Data: the French sports ministry's sports-facilities census (Licence Ouverte 2.0) and OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Every page names its source. A missing field does not mean “no”: it simply was never declared.
