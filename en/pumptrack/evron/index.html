What the town says about it

A third source, neither the ministry census nor OpenStreetMap: what the town itself publishes. It is the only one that says where a facility came from, who built it and what it cost.

Évron's pumptrack was built in the Grands-Prés district: 250 metres of asphalt track for BMX, mountain bike, scooter, roller skates and skateboard. Together with Mayenne's, it is part of a scheme run by the Mayenne Departmental Council under the Héritage Mayenne 2024 programme.1, 2
